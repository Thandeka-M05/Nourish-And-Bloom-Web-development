From One Planter Box to a Rooftop Garden

Nourish and Bloom is an independent wellness café in Sandton Central, built by three friends who wanted city food to taste like it was grown nearby.

Our History

Nourish and Bloom opened in October 2021 as a four-table coffee counter on the second floor of a Maude Street office block. The rooftop was unused, so we filled it with planter boxes and started growing fresh herbs.

By 2023, the terrace became the café. We now seat sixty guests and grow more than forty herb varieties upstairs.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To serve fresh, energising food and drink grown as close to the table as possible in a warm, welcoming environment for everyone in Sandton.

Vision

To become Sandton's most-loved everyday garden—expanding to three rooftop cafés by 2030 while supporting local employment and community training.

Our Values

Grown Here

All herbs, leaves, and edible flowers are harvested daily from our rooftop garden.

Nothing Wasted

Juice press pulp becomes seed crackers, and garden trimmings return to compost beds.

Local Sourcing

Coffee from a local roastery, dairy from Gauteng family farms, and bread baked nearby.
